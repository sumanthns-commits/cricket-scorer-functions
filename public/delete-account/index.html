Delete Your Crease Account

If you still have the app installed

  1. Open Crease and sign in
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm

This takes effect immediately and can't be undone.

If you've already uninstalled the app

Email us from the address associated with your account and we'll process the deletion manually:

nsready@gmail.com

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile, email, and sign-in credentials
  • Your club memberships and any pending join requests
  • Push notification tokens registered to your device

What's kept

Historical match statistics tied to matches you played (runs scored, wickets taken, catches, etc.) remain visible to your club for record-keeping — the same as when any player leaves a club. This history is no longer linked to your personal account or sign-in after deletion.
